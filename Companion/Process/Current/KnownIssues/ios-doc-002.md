## Full detail

**The class, and why it is a class rather than eight typos.** Thirteen instances were confirmed across audit rounds 15 and 16 by six independent auditors and two models, in a tree where **no test can ever go red for any of them**. A doc comment that says *"the two stores"* when there are three, or *"the single epoch comparison in this file"* when there are two, compiles, ships, and reads as verified fact; the next reader uses it to STOP LOOKING. That is the damage: not the wrong integer, the un-taken search.

**The census, reproduced rather than asserted.** `git grep -n -i -P -- '^[[:space:]]*///?[[:space:]].*(the single|the only|the sole|exactly (one|two|three|four|five)|all (two|three|four|five|six|seven|eight)|(two|three|four|five|six|seven|eight|nine|ten) (consumers|stores|sites|callers|arms|paths|writers|places|producers|doors|exits|readers|copies|instances|occurrences))' 7c143daa5 -- TabMail/ Shared/ TabMailNotificationService/` → **503 lines, exit 0**. Non-vacuity control (`'The single epoch comparison'` → `IMAPProvider.swift:4373`) confirms the predicate is live. A broader predicate — adding bare `never`, bare `zero`, bare `both` — returns well over a thousand.

**THE DECISION RULE, which most candidates fail, stated so it can be re-applied rather than re-invented.** A sentence is in the **fixable** subset iff **(a)** it asserts a count or uniqueness of **CODE SITES** — not of states, values, enum cases, data rows, or architectural singletons like SQLite's one writer — **and (b)** it does **not** carry the means to re-derive it in place: it neither enumerates its members by name nor quotes a predicate. Clause (b) is what discriminates, and it is not a stylistic preference: **a sentence that names its members fails LOUDLY** (the named symbol is absent, or is not a caller, and the reader sees it immediately), while **a bare integer fails SILENTLY** and keeps its credibility while being wrong. That is `MIS-031` — *a sentence that enumerates is a cache* — and a cache with no invalidation is a lie with a delay.

**Result: 9 sentence-groups at 12 line locations — 1.8% of the 503, and DISJOINT from the 8 the round's brief named.** Four were FALSE and were corrected: `BodyAssetStore` *"Two callers"* while the same sentence named four (→ four); `BodyAssetStore`'s MARK *"the only bump site is user tap"* (→ three sites, and the true invariant is the PROPERTY — every bump is a user gesture — not the singular); and a matched pair, `AccountManagerActions` *"×1 … ×2 … those THREE sites"* and `SearchView` *"guarding those three sites"*, both carrying the number round 11 (R11-I) had already retracted to **five** — the `AccountManagerActions` one while **pointing the reader at the very block that retracts it**, and the `SearchView` one **twenty lines below its own correction**. Five more were TRUE but unre-derivable and were given predicates rather than edits: `AccountManagerAI` *"these nine sites"* (→ `aiGuardedHeaderWrite`, 9); `SyncEngineFTS` *"six callers"* (→ `removeHeadersFromFTS`, 6); `UserLabelMenuView` *"four call sites"* (→ `Self.supportsRemoteUserLabels(`, 4); the twin *"the SINGLE gate every enqueue site in this actor routes through"* in `ActiveBodyQueue` and `BackfillBodyQueue` (→ `admit(`, 5 enqueue sites each); and `AccountManagerCalendarQueue`'s *"39 raw `print`s"*, which was **already stale at the commit that wrote it** because the same round's FIX-1 removed some.

**Why the other ~491 are accepted, stated as a positive property and not as fatigue.** They bound no search: they are semantic (*"the only bucket a caller may report as success"*), architectural (*"the single GRDB writer"* — SQLite's, not a call-site count), about data (*"finds exactly one remaining exact match"*), historical and past-tense (*"was a bare `25` at three provider call sites"*), about the `v2final` sibling tree rather than this one, or — the large majority — **already compliant**: they enumerate their members inline (`MessageHeaderRekey`'s *"the THREE stores"*, `NSEDataBridge`'s *"All six call sites (deep-link tap, …)"*, `DraftSessionRegistry`'s *"all THREE of `ChatStore`'s consumers (…)"*), or already carry a re-run instruction (`IMAPProvider` *"five sites in this file today; **attack the claim by re-running it**"*, `SearchIndex`'s nine-`FTSHeaderRecord`-sites census *"enumerated by grepping the TYPE rather than any one call shape"*). The convention this row makes standing is therefore **already the tree's majority practice**; what was missing was the rule that says so.

**THE PREDICATE MUST BE AN INSTRUMENT THE SENTENCE CANNOT ENTER (`MIS-033`), and this is not hypothetical.** `AppDatabase.swift`'s `v82` banner carried three counts — *"exactly TWO"*, *"ZERO"*, *"zero"* — each quoting the very `rg` that would return **3, 1 and 1** because **each sentence is a hit for its own pattern**. The fix is a negative lookahead that excludes comment lines, `--pcre2 '^(?!\s*(///|//))'`, **plus a paired non-vacuity run**, because a claimed ZERO hides best and a self-contaminated negative control manufactures confidence. ⚠️ **The countermeasure already existed in that same file, with its own `MIS-033` citation, about 530 lines ABOVE the `v82` banner, and was simply not applied below it** — a documented-but-half-applied guard, which is the `MIS-018` shape (*the unit of a port is the CONTRACT*). **That is the precedent; this row generalises it.**

**Recoverability, with the non-recovering case named (`MIS-IOS-008`).** Every instance is recoverable by re-running its predicate, and nothing in the running system depends on a comment. **The non-recovering case is the one that motivates the row:** a reader who trusts a false count and *therefore does not search* leaves the un-audited site un-audited, and that omission is invisible — it produces no failing test, no warning, and no log line. Round 16's own item 7 exists because round 15 fixed four defects at the instance and not the class.

**What would re-open this row:** a NEW counting sentence about code sites landing without its predicate (that is the convention breaking, not the row aging), or a re-run of the census returning materially more than 503 at a later candidate, which would mean the convention is not being applied to new prose.
