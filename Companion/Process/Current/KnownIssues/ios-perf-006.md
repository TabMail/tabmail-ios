## Full detail

On an upgrade launch (owner device capture 2026-08-06) there is **ONE ~1,320 ms main-thread block immediately after first paint** — not twelve. The twelve descending `[MainStall] 1320…209 ms` lines are that single block draining queued `MainActorStallDetector` timer ticks: the detector schedules a 100 ms repeating timer that stamps `scheduledAt` and dispatches to `DispatchQueue.main`, so during one long block every tick queues behind it and they all drain together, the oldest measuring the whole block. **~606 ms of the window emitted no marks of any kind.** `e386b26aa` adds 13 `BootProfiler.mark` lines around the four live `@MainActor` consumers in that window — `warmUpWebKitIfNeeded()` (the prime suspect: `WKWebView(frame: .zero)` + `loadHTMLString` on the main actor, i.e. a cold WebContent/GPU/Networking process launch, whose 500 ms settle is a `Task.sleep` resuming on the main actor so it was not determinable whether the spin-up landed inside or after the busy window), the `RootView` post-paint setup prologue, `storeKitManager.start()`, and an anchor at the head of `InboxViewModel.listDidAppear()`. The residual after those is **first SwiftUI body evaluation**, left deliberately unmarked because pricing it means instrumenting body evaluation — far noisier than this window justifies. **Why this is a decision and not a deferral:** the block is post-first-paint (the UI is already on screen), one-time per launch, and carries no data loss, no dropped intention and no brick, so under THE MANTRA it is recoverable and registerable; the engineering judgement — instrument first, and do not move WebKit warm-up on a hunch — is made here, and the next device capture attributes the window with marks that are already shipping. **Two sub-findings recorded without change:** (a) `InboxView.onAppear` is **at** the Swift type-checker's expression budget — one added statement fails the build with *"unable to type-check this expression in reasonable time"*, hit twice (interpolated and pre-computed-`Int` forms), which is why the inbox anchor lives in the view model and is a hazard for anyone adding to that closure; (b) `deferredSearchIndexInitIfNeeded` uses a **bare `Task.detached` with no explicit priority**, unlike the EmbeddingService task's explicit `.utility`, so the FTS open runs at an unspecified/effectively-high QoS and competes for device CPU while main renders — it does **not** block main. **And one correction worth carrying:** `SearchIndex.initialize()` is **NOT** main-actor-bound — `SearchIndex` is a plain `actor` with no global-actor annotation, no `MainActor.run` and no `DispatchQueue.main` in the file, reached from `Task.detached`, so its 275 ms Δ is **elapsed time overlapping the first render, not main-thread time**. Both of its marks now say so, because reading that Δ as main-thread cost is precisely the error `MIS-032` was written to prevent.
