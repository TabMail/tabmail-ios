# Subscription trials and signup promises

Owner selected a one-week card-free signup grant plus a fresh one-week subscription trial for eligible Basic/Pro buyers. Backend grant length and App Store Connect offer activation are separate settings; Swift does not activate Apple offers. Zero/BYOK has no introductory offer. This supersedes the offer-removal/forfeiture behavior described as current in the historical portions of ADR-IOS-040 and memory 118, without changing the signup-vs-paid `trialState` discriminator.

`StoreKitManager.freeTrialOffer(for:)` reads the actual product's free introductory offer and eligibility. Cards display that real duration and recurring price. `SubscriptionTrialPurchasePolicy` protects a running signup trial: a purchase is unavailable when its first charge could fall before the existing promised expiry. The purchase boundary refreshes both `/whoami` and Apple eligibility before invoking StoreKit; failed account reads do not become permission. This also protects legacy 14-day signup grants while the new default becomes seven days.

A fresh Apple trial starts at purchase, so signup access and offer overlap when someone commits early. Do not promise an unconditional two weeks after immediate purchase. The accurate general wording is one card-free week plus a fresh week from subscription confirmation, up to two weeks total; the live Apple offer and eligibility determine the actual subscription terms.

`AccountInfo.subscriptionTrialEnd(now:)` describes a purchased Apple/Stripe plan's future free period separately from signup `trialState`. It preserves normal subscriber controls. Apple webhook must emit the existing trial shape from verified signed FREE_TRIAL metadata. Neither an arbitrary configured duration nor `offerType` alone is trial proof.

`PlanPurchaseFlow.purchase` owns the preservation boundary. It refreshes account state, checks the captured identity, reads the real offer, checks identity again, applies the preserved-expiry policy, and only then invokes the purchase. Account-read failure or identity changes do not purchase. `PlanPurchaseFlow.run` is the production orchestration used by `PlanPickerView`: it delegates to that boundary, finishes the purchase UI state, delivers a successful signed purchase once, then checks user and generation before invoking the current-account completion. The view supplies its existing backend/StoreKit/UI effects and retains error/cancellation presentation.

Coverage: `SubscriptionTrialOfferTests`, `AccountInfoTrialStateTests`, `PlanCardCTATests`, `PlanPurchaseFlowTests`, `PlanPurchaseOrchestrationTests`, `TrialStoreKitTests`, `AccountTrialStatusBannerTests`. Run unit tests with the committed `TabMailTests` scheme; it activates local StoreKit while the normal app and screenshot schemes retain real metadata behavior. The StoreKit suite uses a temporary local catalog and real `Product` metadata/eligibility, without changing the scheme catalog or live offers. Each run owns a fresh subscription group, verifies raw catalog identity before buying locally, finishes its transaction and observes consumed eligibility with a bounded wait on the same cached Product, then restores the original catalog in teardown. The future combined release must remain manual-release and coordinate the service, billing, website and Apple cutover, including old clients; this code does not control purchases in older binaries.
